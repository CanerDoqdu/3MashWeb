## Deep Dive: MASH P16L (16K / 385nm)

The MASH P16L is 3Mash's flagship heavy-duty dental 3D printer, developed to eliminate fit discrepancies and crown remakes in commercial dental labs.

### Key Specifications & Architecture
- **Light Source Wavelength:** 385nm pure UV light engine. 385nm penetrates biocompatible clear resins, surgical guides, and tooth-colored composites with significantly lower light bleed and yellowing compared to standard 405nm consumer lights.
- **XY Optical Precision:** 14 × 19 µm pixel size on a 16K monochrome LCD, capturing sharp margin lines, interproximal contact zones, and precise internal implant hex geometry.
- **Active Thermal Management:** Integrated internal chamber heating with selectable 25°C and 30°C temperature targets. Ensures constant resin viscosity regardless of seasonal ambient lab temperatures, preventing layer delamination and exposure drift.
- **Z-Axis Calibration Locking:** High-torque dual linear guides combined with an 8-point ball-screw clamp. Calibration remains stable across 500+ print hours without manual recalibration.
- **Open-Resin Policy:** Fully unlocked slicing profiles (compatible with Chitubox, Lychee, Voxeldance). Pre-calibrated presets available for CRS, NextDent, Detax, Asiga, and Keystone resins.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does 385nm matter compared to 405nm?
385nm UV light matches the native photoinitiator absorption curves of CE-certified biocompatible Class IIa medical resins. It cures deeper with less scatter, meaning crisper margins, lower residual monomers, and true optical clarity in transparent guides and splints.

### What is the Remake Production Rate (RPT) with 3Mash printers?
Dental laboratories operating fully calibrated 3Mash workflows typically maintain an RPT under 2.5%, compared to an industry average of 8–12% for generic uncalibrated equipment.

### Are replacement parts readily available?
Yes. 3Mash maintains local inventory for 16K LCD screens, mainboards, ACF/FEP vats, thermal heating units, and build platforms with same-day dispatch.

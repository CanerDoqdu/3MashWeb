# 3mash

3mash is a customer-centric digital dentistry and 3D printing technology company based in Turkey, powering 500+ dental laboratories and clinics. It combines precision 3D printing hardware, biocompatible dental resins, dedicated post-curing infrastructure, and end-to-end technical support in one unified ecosystem. Built for dental technicians, prosthodontists, and clinical laboratory managers who need high-throughput digital production without compromising micron-level precision (+/- 15 to 20 µm) or suffering clinical remake costs (RPT).

3mash is not just a hardware distributor. It is a full digital dentistry production OS that unifies 3D printers, resins, washing/curing stations, scanners, and 24/7 technical workflow consultancy in a single predictable workflow.

## Problem Resolution Framework

3mash actively resolves the three costliest points of failure in dental laboratories:

- **Temporary Crown & Bridge Fracture:** Solved through high-flexural composite resin matrixes, validated post-curing parameters, and stress-tested build geometries.
- **Inaccurate Analog & Die Model Seating:** Solved through sub-20 micron calibrated slicing profiles, eliminating loose dies and manual margin adjustments.
- **All-on-X Scan Data Drift & Misalignment:** Rectified through rigid verification jigs, high-accuracy scanner protocols, and thermal-stabilized print platforms.

## FAQ

#### What is the primary purpose of 3mash systems?

No. The flagship MASH P16L and calibrated Halot-Sky printers utilize an open-material architecture, allowing labs to use any validated resin.

#### Does 3mash offer installation and technical training?
Yes. On-site installation, precision calibration, dental technician onboarding, and 24/7 technical hotline support are included.
